# 📘 Fellow Notes 2.0

**Fellow Notes 2.0** is the next-generation academic hub designed and engineered by **Team Binary Minds** for BCA students. It delivers organized, semester-wise study materials, authentic previous year question papers (PYQs), and essential student productivity tools in an ultra-fast, modern web interface.

> 🛡️ **Notice on Academic Compliance:**  
> In accordance with institutional guidelines and university academic policies, the practicals section has been retired. Fellow Notes 2.0 focuses exclusively on policy-compliant curriculum notes, verified question banks, and student utilities.

---

## ✨ What's New in Fellow Notes 2.0

- 📱 **Multi-Device Responsive Architecture:** Fully adaptive across phones (iPhone SE, Androids), tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide screens. No horizontal overflow, fluid typography, touch-optimized elements, and an accessible mobile drawer navigation.
- 🌓 **Adaptive Dark / Light Themes:** Sleek, eye-friendly design system with localStorage memory and system preference detection.
- 🔍 **Live Real-time Search (`Ctrl + K`):** Instant filter across all semesters and subjects as you type.
- 📊 **SGPA & CGPA Calculator with Presets:** Auto-load official subjects for all semesters with one click, or enter custom courses with letter grades (`O` to `F`).
- 🎯 **75% Attendance "Bunk or Attend" Planner:** Instant math to find out how many classes you can safely skip, or how many consecutive classes you must attend to maintain the mandatory 75% attendance threshold.
- ⏱️ **Pomodoro Study Room:** Built-in 25/5/15 minute interval timer with synthesized Web Audio chimes and study streak tracker.
- 📚 **Refreshed PYQ Bank:** Past examination papers indexed by semester and session cycles (Winter & Summer).
